## FAQ-001 — Password Reset

**Category**

account_access

**Keywords**

password

forgot password

login

reset password

**Question**

How do I reset my password?

**Approved Answer**

Select **Forgot Password** on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

## FAQ-002 — Change Email Address

**Category**

account_access

**Keywords**

email

change email

profile

account settings

**Question**

Can I change my email address?

**Approved Answer**

Yes.

Open **Account Settings → Profile**, update your email address, and save the changes.

**Category**

**Approved Answer**
