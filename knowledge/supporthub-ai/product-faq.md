\# Product FAQ



\## Purpose



This document contains frequently asked questions that SupportHub AI can use to answer common user requests.



\---



\## Password Reset



\*\*Question:\*\* How do I reset my password?



\*\*Answer:\*\* Select \*\*Forgot Password\*\* on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.



\---



\## Change Email Address



\*\*Question:\*\* Can I change my email address?



\*\*Answer:\*\* Yes. Open \*\*Account Settings → Profile\*\*, update your email address, and save the changes.



\---



\## Download Invoices



\*\*Question:\*\* Where can I download my invoices?



\*\*Answer:\*\* Open \*\*Billing → Invoices\*\* to view or download your available invoices.



\---



\## Contact Support



\*\*Question:\*\* How can I contact support?



\*\*Answer:\*\* Submit a support ticket through the SupportHub portal. A support agent will respond according to the ticket priority and SLA.
